What this is—and is not.

Apex is Salesforce’s Java-like server language and genuine Apex compiles and runs only on the Salesforce Platform. This independent lab teaches syntax and concepts with a deliberately limited simulator. It is not Salesforce, a Salesforce sandbox, or a replacement for platform validation.
