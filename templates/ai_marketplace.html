AI Reviews Intake

AI Reviews receives paired prompts and AI-created outputs. Each eligible daily submission becomes a stocked item in the AI Warehouse, where independent raters help classify what worked and what failed.

The Walmart analogy

AI Reviews receives the loads. AI Warehouse stocks the shelves.

Imagine an empty Walmart: the building and shelves exist, but the people arrive with the products. Here, contributors bring one “product”—an AI prompt paired with its output attachment—and AI Reviews buys the eligible load for one penny of earned credit. AI Warehouse then stocks that item in the catalog.

Third-party raters act like quality inspectors. Their votes move inventory onto shelves such as Listened to the prompt well, Most truthful, Most helpful, Deliberate hallucination, and Misleading. The stocked, assessed catalog becomes licensable evaluation memory that can help measure and improve future model output.

AI Reviews intake → AI Warehouse inventory → human ratings → organized memory → commercial evaluation access.

Walmart is used only as a familiar retail analogy. ThePolka.Cloud is not affiliated with or endorsed by Walmart.

Earned credit: $0.01 per eligible submission, limited to one submission per email per UTC day. The penny is recorded in the contributor ledger. Cash transfer requires identity verification, an activated payout method, tax review where applicable, and the published payout threshold. Do not upload confidential, private, infringing, or regulated data.
