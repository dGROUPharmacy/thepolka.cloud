The Executable Dot: A Distinctive Architecture for a Self-Auditing Python Web Ecosystem

A technical case study of ThePolka.Cloud.

What makes this research different is the alignment of language and infrastructure: the dot in thepolka.cloud simultaneously completes the spoken name “the polka-dot cloud,” serves as the brand mark, and performs real DNS routing. The paper connects that executable identity to a Flask, Gunicorn, GitHub, Render, and Cloudflare architecture designed to leave inspectable operational evidence.

Keywords: Python, Flask, web deployment, self-auditing systems, automation, Render, cloud architecture, design systems.
