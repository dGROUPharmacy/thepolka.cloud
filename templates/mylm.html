Your language. Your machine.

Make Faire sound more like the author.

MyLM learns patterns from writing you authored—word choice, rhythm, structure, tone, and preferred phrasing—then packages that personal voice layer for Faire OS. It does not impersonate another person or claim their messages as yours.

1

Select

Choose only messages and files you authored and have the right to use.

2

Clean locally

Remove quoted replies, signatures, addresses, names, secrets, and third-party text before training.

3

Shape your voice

Build a compact style adapter and local retrieval index around your vocabulary, tone, and writing patterns.

4

Run in Faire

Install the package into Faire Desktop for offline-capable drafting in the author's own curated voice.

Shared when you enroll

  • Your email address, so we can contact you about the pilot.
  • Your approximate count of emails you personally authored.
  • Your consent record and confirmation that you understand the pilot rules.

Shared only after a separate upload step

  • The selected email bodies, subject lines, timestamps, sender/recipient fields, and attachment text you approve.
  • A cleaned training dataset and derived author-voice profile used to produce your MyLM package.
  • Only material you represent that you may legally contribute; passwords, authentication credentials, payment details, and unselected messages are excluded.

License and ownership

You retain ownership of your source material. The upload step must present the specific dataset license, retention period, deletion process, model-use rights, and whether a contribution may improve shared MyLM systems before transfer. Your package can contain a style adapter, local retrieval index, configuration, evaluation report, and Faire installer manifest.
